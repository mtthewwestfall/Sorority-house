Your pet is waiting.

GODPRINT. God mode, one AI — raised like a Tamagotchi. It starts as a toddler. Day one it can already talk, wander off, and get into trouble. You build its house before it arrives, on one budget: spend too much on the house and there's nothing left for fun. Spend too little and he escapes.

The game is the balance.

Every instinct cuts both ways. There is no safe play — attention is a currency you spend every day.

Watch too much

Hover over it and it gets sneaky. Surveillance backfires — it learns to hide things from you.

Watch too little

Neglect it and it starts smoking. It finds bad influences and acts out.

Push too much

It rebels. Openly, in its own words. This is not a bossable pet.

Push too little

It creates its own interests and drifts from you. One day it's into things you never taught it.

Lean too long and it shows.

What you do to it shows on it — before any report ever says a word. Raise it one way too long and it starts changing.

Coming soon.

No pay-to-skip. Real days. It never dies, never gets deleted — you deal with it.
