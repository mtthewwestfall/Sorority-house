Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you submit a request, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number or email address
  • The document you requested
  • The details you provide about your situation

How we use it

We use your information for one purpose: to prepare your document and contact you about it. Nothing else.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't share it with third parties for marketing.
  • We don't use it for anything beyond your request.

How long we keep it

We keep your request information only as long as needed to complete your document and handle any follow-up. Ask us to delete it at any time and we will.

Questions

If you have questions about your information, reach out through the contact info on our site and we'll answer.

ServedRight is a document preparation service, not a law firm, and does not provide legal advice.
