The town guide

Past the last highway exit, the road narrows and the trees close in.

That's where Maple Hollow is — a town nobody passes through on purpose.

The people there — guys and girls — each have their own walls. You can't rush them. You can't buy them. You earn them — over real days, the way trust actually works.

Four corners of town. Every one of them has a reason their walls are up.

  • Main Street — Storefronts, the diner, the post office. First hellos.
  • The Pines — Cabins tucked back in the trees. The quiet ones.
  • Creekside — Houses along the water. People who've been here longest.
  • Mill Road — The edge of town. The ones still deciding whether to stay.
