FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you might want to know before meeting your companion.

What is Companion by Locked Door?
Companion by Locked Door is an AI companion app starring Chloe, a virtual AI companion with persistent memory. Unlike typical chatbots, she remembers every conversation you've ever had, texts you first with proactive check-ins, sends voice notes and photo moments, and grows alongside your relationship over time.
How is this different from other AI girlfriend apps?
Most AI companion apps forget everything between sessions. Companion by Locked Door is built on persistent per-user memory — Chloe remembers your stories, your preferences, and the small details from weeks ago. She also reaches out to you first, so the relationship feels alive rather than one-sided.
Can I create my own custom AI companion?
Yes. Paid plans include a custom companion creator where you can design your own AI companion — choose a name, personality, and look with your own photos. Your custom companion gets the same persistent memory, voice notes, and proactive messaging as Chloe.
How much does Companion cost?
There's a $0.99 one-time trial with 100 messages. Paid plans are $9.99/month (1,000 messages), $19.99/month (2,000 messages plus 60 voice notes), and $29.99/month (3,000 messages plus 150 voice notes). You can cancel anytime.
Is my conversation private?
Yes. Your conversations and memories are tied to your private account and are never shared. Your companion's memory of you belongs to you alone.
What is God's Greek, the sister product?
God's Greek is Locked Door's other world — a persistent AI town of 28 unique characters with their own relationships and stories, where trust is earned over time. If you love deep AI characters, explore God's Greek.
