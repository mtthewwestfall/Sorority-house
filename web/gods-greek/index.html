What is God's Greek?

God's Greek is a persistent AI story world — an interactive AI fiction experience set in a small Southern town deep in the pines. It features 28 unique AI characters who know each other, hold ongoing storylines, and remember how you treat them across every conversation.

How is this different from other AI character chat apps?

Most AI character chat apps give you isolated chatbots that forget you between sessions. God's Greek is a persistent world: the 28 characters know each other, talk about each other, and remember your actions over time. Relationships develop, reputations form, and the story continues whether you're there or not. It's closer to an AI visual novel or story game than a chatbot.

What does "always earned, never given" mean?

Trust is a real system here, not a script. Characters start guarded and open up only as you prove yourself through consistent, genuine interaction. They notice how you treat others, remember slights and kindnesses, and their behavior toward you changes accordingly. Nothing is handed to you — every relationship is earned.

Do the characters remember me between visits?

Yes. God's Greek features persistent memory: characters remember your past conversations, the choices you made, and how you treated them — and each other. The world keeps turning between your visits, and you'll find the story has moved on when you return.
