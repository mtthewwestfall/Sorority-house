Westfall

Storefronts, the diner, the post office. First hellos.

This is where God's Greek shows its face. The diner grill is going by six in the morning, the bakery door chimes all day, and everybody passes everybody else at least twice before noon. If you're new in town, this is where you start — and where everybody gets their first read on you.
