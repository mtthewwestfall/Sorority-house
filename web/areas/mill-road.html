Mill Road

The edge of town. The ones still deciding whether to stay.

Out past the old mill, the pavement gives up and the houses sit farther apart. Mill Road is where you land when you're not sure you're staying — and where some people realize they never want to leave. The doors out here open slow, but they open wide.
