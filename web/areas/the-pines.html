The Pines

Cabins tucked back in the trees. The quiet ones.

Past the last streetlight, the road narrows and the trees close in. The cabins back here sit quiet — woodsmoke, pine needles, nobody in a hurry. The people who live in the Pines didn't come here to be found. Earn your way in slowly, and you'll find the warmest doors in town.
